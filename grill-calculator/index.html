How the eye reckons

Grill-day rules of thumb, not lab facts. A regular man targets about half a pound of cooked meat, a woman a bit less, a kid by age. Appetite nudges that about 15% each way.

The cooked target splits across your proteins by the balance sliders, then grosses up to raw buying weight, because meat shrinks on the fire and bone and shell don't feed anyone. Shellfish needs the most raw weight; sausage, paneer and tofu the least.

Smoker adds ~10% for the long shrink. Sides trims a quarter. Weights round to a tenth of a pound.
