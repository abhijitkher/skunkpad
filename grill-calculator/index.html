How the eye reckons

These are grill-day rules of thumb, not laboratory facts. Tune with the sliders if your crowd runs hungrier.

Each eater gets a target of cooked meat: a regular man about half a pound, a regular woman a bit less, a kid scaled by age. The appetite slider swings that from roughly 60% to 160%.

That cooked target is split evenly across the meats you pick, then grossed up to raw buying weight, because meat loses weight on the fire and bone and shell don't feed anyone. Shellfish needs the most raw weight for that reason; sausage the least.

A smoker adds about 10% for the long, slow shrink. Sides checked trims the whole thing by a quarter. Each meat is rounded to the nearest quarter pound so you can actually order it.
